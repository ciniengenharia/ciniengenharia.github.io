What is NR-12

NR-12 is the Brazilian Ministry of Labour's regulatory standard on the safety of machinery and equipment. It applies to new and used machines, whether built in Brazil or imported. CE marking is not enough: in Brazil, customers and labour inspectors check compliance with NR-12, which has its own requirements.

A non-compliant machine can be stopped at the plant by the customer or by the labour inspectorate. Adapting it before shipping costs far less than modifying it in Brazil.
