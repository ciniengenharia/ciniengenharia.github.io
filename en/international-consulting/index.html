Why you need consulting in Brazil

Brazil has its own safety rules, with the force of law: the Ministry of Labour's Regulatory Standards (NR-10 for electricity, NR-12 for machinery) and the ABNT NBR technical standards. A foreign company, however well organised, often does not know which requirements apply to its case, how they are enforced and who may sign the documents.

The consequences show up in practice: machines sold that the Brazilian customer cannot put into operation, contracts with safety clauses nobody fully understood, branches that discover non-conformities during an inspection. Consulting from the start costs far less than fixing things afterwards.

What Brazilian law says

NR-12 does not only concern machine users: it also applies to the manufacture, import, sale and exhibition of machines in Brazil. NR-10 applies to all activities involving electrical installations and services. Technical documents must be signed by a professional registered with CREA, with an ART (Law 6.496/1977).
