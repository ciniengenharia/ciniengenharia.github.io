Why NR-12 matters

NR-12 is one of the standards most frequently enforced by Brazilian labour inspectors. It has its own requirements for guards, safety devices, controls, emergency stops, manuals and training. CE marking is not enough: in Brazil, what counts is compliance with NR-12.

A non-compliant machine can lead to fines, civil and criminal liability after an accident, and the machine being shut down.

What Brazilian law says

NR-12 (Machinery and equipment) applies to all machines used in Brazil, new or used, made in the country or imported. It is backed by arts. 184–186 of the CLT. Where there is serious and imminent risk, labour inspectors can shut the machine down (NR-3).
