Why it matters in Brazil

Industrial installations grow by adaptation: new machines, new panels, added circuits. After a few years nobody knows whether the protection is still adequate. The inspection compares what is installed with what Brazilian standards require today.

It is often requested by insurers, customers, headquarters audits and labour inspectors.

What Brazilian law says

Every engineering design, inspection or report in Brazil must be registered with an ART (Law 6.496/1977) by a professional registered with CREA (Law 5.194/1966). NR-10 also requires every organisation to keep the electrical design and records of earthing inspection and measurement (item 10.15.3).

What it covers

  • service entrance, distribution panels and circuit identification;
  • conductors, protective devices and shock protection;
  • earthing and equipotential bonding;
  • physical condition, IP rating, barriers and signage;
  • points with arc flash risk.

What you receive

  • technical report with photos of each non-conformity and standard reference;
  • priority matrix, separating what is urgent from what can be scheduled;
  • action plan with deadlines;
  • ART registered with CREA.
