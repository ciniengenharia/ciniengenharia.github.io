Arc flash and ATPV

How dangerous each panel is, and which clothing is really needed.

Why it matters in Brazil

NR-10 requires protective clothing to be suited to the arc flash risk. The protection value (ATPV) is not chosen from a catalogue: it depends on the incident energy at each panel, calculated under ABNT NBR 17227 and IEEE 1584.

What Brazilian law says

Every engineering design, inspection or report in Brazil must be registered with an ART (Law 6.496/1977) by a professional registered with CREA (Law 5.194/1966). NR-10 also requires every organisation to keep the electrical design and records of earthing inspection and measurement (item 10.15.3).

What it covers

  • incident energy at each work point;
  • arc flash boundary;
  • measures to reduce the risk, before clothing.

What you receive

  • study with calculation report;
  • panel labels;
  • mitigation plan;
  • ART registered with CREA.
