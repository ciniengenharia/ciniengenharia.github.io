Cini Engenharia

Cini Engenharia is an electrical and occupational safety engineering firm based in Rio Grande do Sul, in southern Brazil. The region, between the Serra Gaúcha and Greater Porto Alegre, is one of the country's main industrial hubs: metalworking, automotive, furniture, food, chemicals.

We work for companies and industries: design and inspection of electrical installations, the documentation and inspections required by NR-10 and NR-12, team training, and software to keep compliance up to date.

What Brazilian law says

In Brazil the engineering profession is governed by Law 5.194/1966 and may only be practised by professionals registered with the state CREA. Law 6.496/1977 created the ART: every engineering job must be registered under the name of the engineer in charge.

The engineer in charge

Dario Cini is an electrical engineer and occupational safety engineer, graduated from the University of Caxias do Sul, with more than 10 years in industrial electrical engineering.

The double qualification matters: whoever signs an NR-10 or NR-12 document must understand both the electrical installation and the safety of the people working on it.
